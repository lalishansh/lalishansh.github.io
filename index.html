Why this works

  • _index.md at the root is the home page, rendered by _layouts/home.html.
  • Every page kind needs its own template: home.html, section.html and page.html, all wrapped by baseof.html.
  • Everything that is not content (_layouts/, _build/, hugo.yaml) is listed under ignoreFiles, otherwise Hugo would try to turn it into pages.
